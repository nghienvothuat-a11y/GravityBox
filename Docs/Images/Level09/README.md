# 09 — Native macOS review

Captured from the Unity macOS player on 2026-09-08 with F12, at native 796 × 1494 resolution. These are real rendered game states, not mockups.

- [Closed.png](Closed.png): level09 at spawn; amber slider blocks the passage, holding recess and cover-mounted guide are visible.
- [Open.png](Open.png): after native drag input tilts the box, the slider moves into its housing under world gravity and the passage becomes clear. The ball also moves naturally; it is not parked or captured by a script.

The selector was scrolled to09 and the level loaded through its card. Native reset restored the original box, ball and slider poses. These views verify presentation, opening and reset, not a complete human solve. The full spawn-to-escape route is covered separately by the rotation-only PlayMode test.
