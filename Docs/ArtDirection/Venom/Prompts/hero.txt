Use case: stylized-concept.
Project: Venom, an original mobile physics puzzle about guiding one living material creature through a rotatable six-face glass cube. Create original production-quality stylized 3D concept art, sophisticated tactile toy aesthetics. Not Marvel.
Core identity: a faceless asymmetric living ink mass, charcoal indigo #252938 with broad soft violet sheen #8586BD, continuous folded soft body and a thin adhesive skirt, four irregular freely raised tapered curling tendrils on a whole body, fewer on a fragment. Absolutely NO eyes, NO mouth, NO head or neck, NO cute animal anatomy, NO evenly spaced octopus legs. Intention and charm come from asymmetrical leaning, curling and gestures. Broad highlights, soft satin wet elastomer; no gritty slime, veins, gore, dripping mess.
World palette: warm cream #F4EFE6, pale mineral mint #CBDDD2 and peach #EDB390, mechanism graphite #3C4B51. Action feedback teal #178978 with white symbol; warm amber #EAAA4E for incomplete mechanism; selection periwinkle #777CD9 with three notches. Matte injection-molded shell components, lightly frosted clear acrylic faces, tiny brushed-metal functional pivots. Dark creature clearly separated from light walkable planes. Empty pale warm background, large restrained forms, clean soft studio lighting and ambient contact shadow, crisp at phone size.
Gameplay invariants: creature sticks to inner floor, walls and ceiling and can deform, split and fuse. Ceiling skin sags down in world gravity; attachment stays on solid surface. A single circular exit is a real HOLE through a cube face, never a portal on a vertical display. All matter must physically leave. All six faces exist; camera-facing faces fade almost invisible for readability, not permanently removed from the cube. Rounded visual shell must not cover paths or pretend obstacles. Selection UI is outside the body, never a change of creature mass or material. No money, hearts currency, stars, timer, joystick, shopping icons, power bars, combat, giant particles or fake gameplay. Text minimal and impeccable.
Asset type: vertical 9:16 mobile game art target, portrait 1080x1920 composition, not a phone photograph.
Primary request: a polished playable-looking concept of existing Journey level 05, two unequal portions coordinating on matter sensors. Full level fits without clipping, comfortable whitespace above and below, cube occupies 70% of screen width and 56% of height at center. Elevated orthographic three-quarter view. Keep technical construction simple and practical for mobile.
Geometry: one cubic chamber, a cream inset square floor with just two low flush square sensors, A toward left rear, B toward right rear, each surrounded by a thin ring; a compact powered vertical splitting blade on two mint guide rails at front-middle of floor. Blade parked UP, warm peach cutting edge, no creature under it. One circular exit cut through the center of the translucent CEILING, with a thin teal rim FLUSH to ceiling. The square sliding cover beside the ceiling hole is retracted. The protective lid above B is raised on two visible slim guides. No floor hole.
Exact scene state: a small charcoal creature fragment, 30 g, spread and attached to A; a much larger charcoal fragment, 66 g, spread and attached to B. Same material, sizes correspond to roughly 1:2.2 volumes; both look alive with irregular little tendrils. Each is sitting entirely in its own sensor zone, not joined by any ribbon or tendril. Both sensors are active; existing shared hold has finished; exit cover retracted, but both fragments are still INSIDE. B fragment selected with a slim lavender ring and three clear radial notches. No third creature, no floating matter.
UI layout: at top small uppercase "VENOM" with "05" below, a modest pause icon upper-right. Below title the single sentence "Each part has a purpose". Above respective sensors small readable callouts "A · 30 / 24 g" and "B · 66 / 60 g", with check mark symbols. Above cube ceiling unobtrusive "EXIT OPEN". Bottom HUD in calm cream cards: small line "OUT 0 / 96 g", two adjacent selectable chips "30 g · HOLD A" and "66 g · HOLD B" (B outlined lavender), then one wide teal action pill with EXACT text "EVERYONE OUT". At very bottom small "Tap to guide · Drag to rotate". A thin secondary icon for reunion may sit beside chips but no additional text. Beautiful legible rounded typography, safe margins. This is an art direction proposal, not an actual engine screenshot.
Avoid extra floor tiles, maze paths, belts, pipes, scenery, foliage, creatures on roof, floating platforms, jeweled gates. Preserve clean cube silhouette and causal A to B to exit relation.
