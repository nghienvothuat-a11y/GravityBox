## Verified route

1. Tap blade I; the real cut creates two parts. Select the left part for A.
2. Select the other part and tap the first transfer mouth. In the G/B view,
   tap G directly, then tap above its rail to lift it into the end catch.
3. Release G; tap blade II. Move the right cut part toward the second transfer
   mouth and through the pipe to the C room.
4. Select the remaining central part and **tap B directly**. A remains loaded.
5. Select the rightmost part, grasp C and pull right. Both reunion doors open.
6. Release C. Send each part to the visible floor in the right room. Wait for
   one reunited body, grasp H and pull along its rail, release, then tap the exit.

The initial screen-test draft copied two intermediate world coordinates from
the older direct-command solution. These were poor screen targets: the floor
below the pipe lip was occluded by `Mouth pedestal face`, and the rear-left
central floor was occluded by `Solid middle divider`. The real ray picker
correctly selected the nearer surfaces. They were not evidence of an impossible
level. Removing those unnecessary intermediate taps and choosing the visible G/B
controls completed the route without gameplay changes.
