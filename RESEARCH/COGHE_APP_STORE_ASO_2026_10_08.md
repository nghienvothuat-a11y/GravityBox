# COghe — App Store ASO and launch compliance (US)

Research date: 2026-10-08. All Apple and Google pages were read on this date. Apple's screenshot spec page returned `Last-Modified: Tue, 06 Oct 2026`, and the App Review Guidelines say "Last Updated: June 8, 2026".

Conventions:
- **[Apple]** / **[Google]**: first-party documentation.
- **[3rd party]**: vendor blog, press, or forum post.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no primary source confirms it, or sources disagree.
- **Interpretation**: my reading of the sources, not a quote.

Related local notes: `RESEARCH/COGHE_MONETIZATION_SOURCES_2026_10_02.md`, `PLANS/COGHE_MONETIZATION_CODEX_2026_10_02.md`, `RESEARCH/COGHE_APP_ICON_FORMAT_2026_10_07.md`.

## 0. Decision summary

1. **Screenshots.** Apple's current spec page names two required sets: iPhone "Dynamic Island (medium display)" (1206×2622 or 1179×2556) and iPad 13" (2064×2752 or 2048×2732) if the app supports iPadOS. The same page still calls the 6.5" set required when no 6.9" set is uploaded, and third-party 2026 guides still say "6.9" required". **The safe choice is to upload three portrait sets:** 6.9" at 1320×2868, 6.3" at 1206×2622, and iPad 13" at 2064×2752. The COghe Unity project currently targets iPhone + iPad (`targetDevice: 2`), so the iPad set is mandatory unless the build switches to iPhone-only. Each set allows 1–10 images in JPEG or PNG with no alpha channel.
2. **iPhone Duo.** Screenshots for iPhone Duo become mandatory for submissions from **April 2027**. They are not needed for a 2026 launch.
3. **App previews.** Up to 3 per device size per language, 15–30 s, ≤30 fps, H.264 at 10–12 Mbps or ProRes 422 HQ, stereo AAC 256 kbps, ≤500 MB. Use 886×1920 portrait for every modern iPhone class and 1200×1600 for iPad. Only in-app screen capture is allowed, though narration and text overlays are fine.
4. **Text limits.**

   | Field | Limit | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | Name | 2–30 chars | |
   | Subtitle | 30 chars | |
   | Keywords | 100 bytes | comma-separated, no spaces |
   | Promotional text | 170 chars | not used for search ranking |
   | Description | 4000 chars | not part of App Store text relevance; Apple says it is used for web-engine search |
   | What's New | 4000 chars | not shown for v1 |

   Search text relevance uses the title, subtitle, keywords and category. Developer name is also searchable. App Tags (US only) are generated by an LLM from en-US metadata.
5. **Product Page Optimization (PPO).** Up to 3 treatments, one test at a time, up to 90 days. It only works once the app is live. Alternate icons must already be inside the live binary.
6. **Custom Product Pages (CPP).** Up to 70, each with its own URL. They can be attached to organic keywords and opened with deep links on iOS 18+. **Google Ads App campaigns are not documented to support iOS CPPs.** Google's only "custom store listing" feature is Android-only (open beta, Display Network inventory). Treat CPP as an Apple Ads, organic-search and owned-link tool, not a Google Ads one (UNVERIFIED that no hidden support exists; ask a Google rep).
7. **Privacy and tracking.**
   - **Privacy policy URL:** required both in App Store Connect and inside the app.
   - **App Privacy label:** the AdMob + Firebase Analytics + IAP mix needs at least these data types: Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, Coarse Location, Purchase History, and Crash, Performance and Other Diagnostic data (details in §5.2).
   - **ATT:** required **if** the app tracks (for example, IDFA-based personalized ads). If the label says "used to track", Apple rejects builds that do not show the ATT prompt.
   - **SKAdNetwork:** the Google Mobile Ads (GMA) Unity plugin 11.5.0 ships Google's 50 SKAdNetwork IDs.
   - **New ads rule (2.5.18):** apps with ads must let users report inappropriate ads.
8. **Build requirement.** Builds must use Xcode 26 / iOS 26 SDK (since 2026-04-28) and a minimum deployment target of iOS 13 or later (since 2026-09-09). Unity 6000.3.19f1 ships a UnityFramework privacy manifest automatically.
9. **Age rating.** The tiers are now 4+/9+/13+/16+/18+. The questionnaire deadline (2026-01-31) has passed and is now simply part of setup. "Advertising" is a declared capability that still allows 4+. COghe is likely 4+, or 9+ if any cartoon violence is declared.
10. **Kids.** Do **not** choose "Made for Kids" while using AdMob + Firebase Analytics, because Guidelines 1.3 and 5.1.4 effectively bar third-party ads and analytics. The Made for Kids choice is irreversible once approved. Even outside the Kids Category, a cute animated game can count as "directed to children" or "mixed audience" under COPPA (amended rule enforceable since 2026-04-22). Decide the audience before submission.
11. **US state age-assurance laws.** Texas (from 2026-06-04), Utah (2026-05-06) and Louisiana (2026-07-01) now impose obligations on developers through Apple's Declared Age Range API and Significant Change API. Review these with counsel before a US launch.
12. **ASO.** Keyword volume is not available without paid tools. Apple Ads Advanced shows a free 1–5 "search popularity" score per keyword. The suggested positioning is "physics puzzle + virtual pet" with a cute-blob angle (draft metadata in §6.4).

## 1. Screenshots

### 1.1 Count, format, scaling
- "You must include at least one screenshot and can upload up to 10 screenshots per device size in .jpeg, .jpg, or .png formats." **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications
- "Images can't include alpha channels or transparencies." **[Apple]** (same page)
- Scaling: if the UI is the same across sizes and localizations, "you only need to provide screenshots for the highest required resolution. App Store Connect automatically scales them down for smaller device sizes." **[Apple]** (same page)
- Removing a screenshot or preview also removes it from every other display size that uses a scaled copy. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/upload-app-previews-and-screenshots

### 1.2 Required device classes (current Apple text)
Apple's spec page, "Required device sizes":
> "iPhone: At least one screenshot for iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)."
> "iPad (if your app supports iPadOS): At least one screenshot for iPad 13-inch display."

**[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications

**Inconsistency (UNVERIFIED which rule App Store Connect enforces):**
- The same page labels "iPhone with Face ID (large display)" (the old 6.5" class) "Required if app runs on iPhone and screenshots for iPhone with Dynamic Island (large display) aren't provided".
- It also says each class falls back to scaled screenshots from a neighbouring class.
- Third-party 2026 guides still say the 6.9" iPhone set and the 13" iPad set are the only required sets. **[3rd party]** https://screenkit.tools/specs/app-store-screenshot-sizes

**Practical rule:** upload 6.9" (1320×2868) **and** 6.3" (1206×2622) portrait sets made from the same layouts, plus iPad 13" (2064×2752). That satisfies either reading of the rules.

**iPad.** Screenshots are required only "if your app supports iPadOS" / "Required if app runs on iPad". **[Apple]** (same page)
- COghe project observation: `REPOS/GravityBox/ProjectSettings/ProjectSettings.asset` has `targetDevice: 2`, i.e. Unity "iPhone + iPad". iPad 13" screenshots are therefore mandatory with the current setting.
- Switching to iPhone-only would remove the iPad screenshot requirement. Interpretation: whether that is worth it is a product decision, since iPad users then get the iPhone layout.

**iPhone Duo (foldable, ships 2026-10-23).** "Starting April 2027, any apps or games submitted will need to include screenshots for iPhone Duo." **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo (2026-10-05). Not needed for the first launch if it ships before April 2027.

### 1.4 Content rules (frames, marketing text, overlays)
- **2.3.3.** "Screenshots should show the app in use, and not merely the title art, login page, or splash screen. They may also include text and image overlays (e.g. to demonstrate input mechanisms, such as an animated touch point…)". So marketing captions and overlays are allowed. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- **2.3.2.** If screenshots show items that need extra purchases (paid cosmetics, the two non-consumable IAPs), the screenshots must "clearly indicate whether any featured items… require additional purchases." **[Apple]** (same)
- **2.3.7.** Metadata "such as app names, subtitles, screenshots, and previews should not include prices, terms, or descriptions that are not specific to the metadata type." **[Apple]** (same)
- **2.3.8.** Screenshots must suit a 4+ audience even if the app is rated higher, and "Use of terms like 'For Kids' and 'For Children' in app metadata is reserved… for the Kids Category." **[Apple]** (same)
- **2.3.10.** No "names, icons, or imagery of other mobile platforms", so no Android devices and no Google Play badges in shots. **[Apple]** (same)
- **Device frames.** Not prohibited by the Review Guidelines, and top competitors use iPhone frames (Bruno, §6.5).
  - Apple's marketing guidelines say to "Use Apple-provided product bezels… Always use the latest-generation devices" and to use them "as is and without modification… You can place promotional copy… beside the product images, not on top of them." **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
  - Interpretation: if frames are used, use Apple's official current bezels without modification. Frameless full-bleed shots also avoid the issue entirely.
- **Search results.** "the first one to three images will appear in search results when no app preview is available, so make sure these highlight the essence of your app." **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/

### 1.5 New optional "creative assets" (2026)
Product-page header and search-result assets are a new placement. They "are rich images or videos that showcase your app's or game's overarching value, brand… distinct from the in-use functionality requirement in app previews and screenshots." **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/upload-app-previews-and-screenshots

| Placement | Image specs | Video specs |
|---|---|---|
| Header | 21:9 at 3840×1646 (jpg/png), or 16:9 at 5244×2950 (png) | 5–30 s, 30/60 fps, muted by default |
| Search results | 3:2 between 1920×1280 and 3840×2560, or 16:9 at 5244×2950 (png) | 5–30 s, 30/60 fps; cannot be unmuted in search results |

Source for the specs: **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/creative-assets-specifications

No source says these assets are required; treat them as optional (Interpretation). They can be submitted on their own through Asset Library. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-your-app-store-assets

### 1.6 Localization
- Screenshots and previews can be localized per language. If a localized preview is missing, "the app preview in the next best available language" is shown. **[Apple]** screenshot and preview spec pages.
- The US storefront's default language is English (US). The additional supported languages are Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), French, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Mexico) and Vietnamese. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations
- A Vietnamese localization is therefore visible to US users whose device language is Vietnamese.

## 3. Text metadata

| Field | Limit / rule | Indexed for App Store search? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| App name | 2–30 characters | **Yes** (title) | [Apple] https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information ; https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/ |
| Subtitle | ≤ 30 characters; must follow metadata rules; no references to other apps or unverifiable claims (2.3.7) | **Yes** | same + guidelines |
| Keywords | "up to 100 bytes"; each keyword "greater than two characters"; competitor/company names not allowed | **Yes** | [Apple] https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/platform-version-information |
| Promotional text | ≤ 170 characters; editable without a new submission | **No**: "promotional text doesn't affect your app's search ranking" | [Apple] platform-version page; https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/ |
| Description | ≤ 4000 characters, plain text, no HTML; "will be used for web engine search results" | Not in Apple's App Store text-relevance list | [Apple] platform-version page; search page |
| What's New | ≤ 4000 characters; "isn't available for the first version"; required afterwards | No | [Apple] platform-version page |
| Category | Primary + optional secondary; games get up to 2 subcategories | **Yes**: "Your primary category and optional secondary category are indexed" | [Apple] https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/ |
| Developer / company name | — | **Yes**: "Your app is searchable by app name and company name" | [Apple] platform-version page |
| IAP display name / description | 35 / 55 characters; up to 20 promoted items | Promoted IAPs "can also appear in search results" | [Apple] https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/ |
| App Tags | Generated from en-US metadata; US only; you can only deselect tags | Shown in search results and as clickable entities | [Apple] https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-tags ; search page |

Apple's ranking statement: results use "text relevance (matches for your app's title, subtitle, keywords, and primary category), as well as user behavior (downloads, ratings and reviews, and more)." **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/

**Keyword-field rules** (all **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/ unless noted):
- 100 characters, "terms separated by commas and no spaces". Spaces are allowed inside a phrase, e.g. `Property,House,Real Estate`.
- "Don't repeat any words included in your app name, subtitle, or category."
- Plurals count as duplicates: "'climbs' and 'climb,' … are considered duplicates".
- Avoid generic terms ("app", "game"), filler words ("the", "to"), and special characters.
- Rejection risks: unauthorized trademarks or celebrity names, irrelevant terms, "Competing app names", offensive terms.
- 2.3.7: no packing metadata "with trademarked terms, popular app names, pricing information". **[Apple]** guidelines.

**Third-party claims (UNVERIFIED by Apple):**
- *Screenshot captions are OCR-indexed since June 2025.* Appfigures reported this. Its own and other tests found the effect weak; captions reinforce rather than create rankings. **[3rd party]** https://appfigures.com/resources/guides/app-store-algorithm-update-2025
  - Cheap hedge: put the main keywords in large top-of-screen captions.
- *Cross-localization.* The US storefront reportedly indexes keywords from secondary locales such as Spanish (Mexico), which would add keyword space. **[3rd party]** https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/how-to-benefit-from-cross-localization-on-the-app-store
  - Apple documents only which languages a storefront supports (§1.6), not that they are indexed.
  - Interpretation: putting English keywords into the es-MX keyword field is a gray-area tactic. Keep it relevant.

### 4.3 Can a CPP be the destination of a Google Ads App campaign on iOS?
- **No documented support found.** Google's "Ads-targeted Custom Store Listings" are "only available for App campaign for installs (ACi) promoting Android apps". "Inventory is currently limited to the Google Display Network", and the feature is an open beta. **[Google]** https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16742242
- Searches of Google Ads Help for iOS "custom product page" support returned nothing.
- App campaigns for iOS point to the app's App Store listing. **Status: UNVERIFIED negative.** Confirm with a Google Ads rep before planning CPP-specific Google creative.
- Interpretation: Apple's own doc says ad networks can show a CPP only in StoreKit-rendered ads where the ad network passes the CPP identifier. Advertisers do not control that in Google App campaigns.
- Google's iOS App campaign guidance, useful for the launch plan **[Google]** https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10384955 :
  - consolidate to 8 or fewer iOS App campaigns;
  - set up a SKAdNetwork conversion-value schema;
  - consider ATT, because accepted prompts increase observed conversions;
  - use on-device conversion measurement.

## 5. Compliance before launch (Firebase Analytics + AdMob + 2 non-consumable IAPs)

### 5.1 Privacy policy URL
- Required for iOS apps in App Store Connect. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information
- Guideline 5.1.1(i): every app needs the link "in the App Store Connect metadata field and within the app in an easily accessible manner".
- The policy must say:
  - what data is collected and how it is used;
  - that third parties (analytics, ad networks, SDKs) give equal protection;
  - the retention and deletion policy;
  - how to revoke consent or request deletion.

  **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/

### 5.2 App Privacy ("nutrition label")
Apple's rules **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/ :
- Declare data collected by "you and/or your third-party partners", including SDKs.
- "Collect" means sent off the device and kept longer than real-time servicing needs.
- IP address must be declared "based on how you use IP address, such as precise location, coarse location, device ID, or diagnostics".
- "Tracking" means linking app data with third-party data for targeted ads or ad measurement, or sharing it with data brokers.

What the SDKs say they collect:
- **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** **[Google]** https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure (Unity version: https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy/data-disclosure):
  - IP address ("may be used to estimate the general location");
  - non-user-related crash logs;
  - user-associated performance data (launch time, hang rate, energy);
  - a Device ID ("advertising identifier or other app- or developer-bounded device identifiers") for third-party advertising and analytics;
  - Advertising Data;
  - other product interactions (app launch taps, video views).
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** **[Google]** https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10285841 :
  - an automatically assigned app instance ID;
  - general location derived from masked IP (no Location Services);
  - in-app purchase events via StoreKit (product ID, name, price);
  - lifecycle events (screen views, sessions).
  - Extra disclosures can be triggered by: setting a user ID, Google signals, linking AdSupport.framework, linking Google Ads with key events, or data sharing settings.
- Firebase says label accuracy is the developer's responsibility. **[Google]** https://firebase.google.com/docs/ios/app-store-data-collection

**Suggested COghe label.** Interpretation, conservative, assuming no account system, no user ID, and no Crashlytics. Verify against the Xcode privacy report.

| Apple data type | From | Purposes | Linked to user? | Used for tracking? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Location → Coarse Location | AdMob (IP-based), GA (masked IP) | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Conservative: yes | Yes if ATT is granted and personalized ads are served |
| Identifiers → Device ID | AdMob (IDFA/IDFV), GA app instance ID | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Yes | Yes (IDFA) if ATT is used |
| Purchases → Purchase History | GA automatic IAP events | Analytics | Yes (tied to the instance ID) | No |
| Usage Data → Product Interaction | GA events, AdMob interactions | Analytics, Third-Party Advertising | Yes | Yes for the AdMob part if ATT is used |
| Usage Data → Advertising Data | AdMob | Third-Party Advertising, Analytics | Yes | Yes if ATT is used |
| Diagnostics → Crash Data, Performance Data, Other Diagnostic Data | AdMob (and Crashlytics if added) | Analytics, App Functionality, Third-Party Advertising | Crash: AdMob says "non-user related"; Performance: "user-associated" | No |

Also declare **Gameplay Content** if game saves or progress ever leave the device. **[Apple]** app-privacy-details page ("Your app includes game saves… Declare Gameplay Content").

### 5.3 Privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy)
- The manifest records collected data types, tracking status and domains (`NSPrivacyTracking`, `NSPrivacyTrackingDomains`), and required-reason API use. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy-manifest-files
- SDKs on Apple's list must include a manifest, plus a signature when shipped as binaries, in new apps.
  - The list includes UnityFramework, FirebaseCore, FirebaseInstallations, FirebaseCrashlytics, GoogleUtilities, GoogleDataTransport, nanopb, FBLPromises and Promises.
  - Source: **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/
- Approved reasons for the listed APIs have been required for uploads since 2024-05-01. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/
- **Unity.** From 2021.3.35f1, 2022.3.18f1 and 2023.2.7f1, Unity auto-declares the engine's required-reason APIs (file timestamp, user defaults, system boot time, disk space). Developers must add their own `Assets/Plugins/iOS/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` for other uses and must make sure plugins carry manifests. **[3rd party: Unity docs]** https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/apple-privacy-manifest-policy.html
  - COghe uses Unity 6000.3.19f1, which is newer than those versions (Interpretation: covered).
- **AdMob.** iOS GMA SDK 11.2.0+ supports privacy manifests. **[Google]** https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

### 5.4 App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
- **When.** On iOS 14.5+ you need ATT permission "in order to track them or access their device's advertising identifier". Without it the IDFA is all zeros. **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/
  - Apps may not gate features on, or reward, allowing tracking (5.1.2(i)).
  - An explainer screen before the prompt is allowed if it is transparent.
- **`NSUserTrackingUsageDescription`.** Required whenever the app uses the ATT framework; "Your app crashes if it attempts to use the [framework] without including the key". **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/nsusertrackingusagedescription
- **AdMob.** ATT is optional ("If you decide to include App Tracking Transparency"). Google recommends waiting for the ATT callback before loading ads so the IDFA can be used. **[Google]** https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/strategies
  - The UMP SDK can show an IDFA explainer.
  - Unity: use Unity's "iOS 14 Advertising Support" package, or UMP. **[Google]** https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy/strategies
- **Label consistency.** Apps whose App Privacy answers say "used to track" without showing ATT are rejected under 5.1.2 ("The app privacy information you provided… indicates you collect data in order to track the user… However, you do not use App Tracking Transparency…"). **[Apple forums, anecdotal]** https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/701508 ; https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/688582
- **Consent.** UMP (Google consent SDK) should be updated every launch, with a privacy-options entry point if required and a `CanRequestAds()` check. **[Google]** https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/privacy
- **EU only, FYI.** From iOS 27.2 an alternative ATT prompt exists in the EU. Not relevant to the US. **[Apple]** user-privacy page.

### 5.6 Other ad rules (Guideline 2.5.18)
- Ads only in the main binary.
- Ads must be appropriate for the age rating.
- Users must be able to see all information used to target them.
- No targeting based on kids' data.
- Interstitials must be clearly marked ads with "easily accessible and visible close/skip buttons".

### 5.7 Age rating (2025 system)
- Announced 2025-07-24: new 13+, 16+ and 18+ tiers alongside 4+ and 9+. New required questions cover in-app controls, capabilities, medical/wellness and violent themes. Answers were due by **2026-01-31** "to avoid interruption when submitting app updates". **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ks775ehf ; https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/

### 5.8 If COghe targets kids
- **Kids Category (1.3).**
  - No links out, purchases or other distractions except behind a parental gate.
  - "Kids Category apps may not send personally identifiable information or device information to third parties."
  - Apps "should not include third-party analytics or third-party advertising". Narrow exceptions: analytics without the IDFA or identifiable data; contextual ads from services with "publicly documented practices… that include human review of ad creatives".
  - Source: **[Apple]** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- **5.1.4.** Apps "intended primarily for kids should not include third-party analytics or third-party advertising", with the same narrow exceptions, and must comply with COPPA. **[Apple]** same.
- **AdMob in the Kids Category.** Developers report rejections under 1.3 for missing human-review ad policies and for referencing the AdSupport/IDFA APIs. **[Apple forums, anecdotal]** https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/741173 ; https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/133834
  - Interpretation: AdMob + Firebase Analytics is not viable in the Kids Category. COghe should **not** select "Made for Kids".
- **COPPA (US law, applies outside the Kids Category too).**
  - The amended rule took effect 2025-06-23, with compliance required by **2026-04-22**. **[3rd party, law firm]** https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/unpacking-ftcs-coppa-amendments-what-you-need-know ; https://bbbprograms.org/media/insights/blog/coppa-amended
  - Key change: separate verifiable parental consent is needed before disclosing children's data to third parties for non-integral purposes such as targeted ads.
  - The FTC judges "directed to children" on the totality of factors, including "animated characters or child-oriented activities". A mixed-audience service that targets children as one audience is covered. **[FTC]** https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions
  - COghe's cute big-eyed character and pet care make an audience decision necessary.
- **AdMob controls for child or under-age traffic.**
  - `ageRestrictedTreatment` replaces the deprecated `tagForChildDirectedTreatment` / `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent`; child treatment disables IDFA transmission.
  - `maxAdContentRating` can be set to General.
  - Source: **[Google]** https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/targeting
  - Interpretation: a general-audience launch plus a neutral age screen, with child treatment for under-13s, is the usual mixed-audience pattern. Confirm with counsel.
