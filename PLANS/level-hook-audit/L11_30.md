# COghe level audit: play positions L11–L30

2026-10-05 · repo GravityBox (branch NewGraphic) · read-only audit. No repo file was changed, Unity was not run, and no state-changing git command was used.

**Scope.** Positions 11–30 of `VenomCampaignBuilder.SpatialOrder` (`Assets/_Game/Editor/COgheSpatialCampaignBuilder.cs:22-27`).
**Lens.** Producer playtest: new players were not hooked. They found no "wow", weak progression, levels that repeat the previous one, and no moment where they feel clever.

**Conventions**
- Levels are named by play position, for example "L13 (E01)". "Content NN" means scene `COgheSpatialNN`.
- **Taps** come from the native-Mac author replay in 50-level order (`Docs/Verification/COgheSpatialPlus/mac-native-run.json`). They include part selection and the author's re-taps, so they over-count real decisions. `PLACEMENT.md` says the same.
- **Decisions** is my own count of meaningful choices.
- The Spatial 11–30 screenshots (`Docs/Verification/COgheSpatialNext20/NN-*.png`) were taken before the 50-level reorder. The number shown on screen is the content number, not the play position. For example, `13-start.png` shows "13 · Thùng đi thang", which is now L28.
- Claims I could not confirm directly in code or screenshots are marked **[inference]**.

## 0. Executive summary: the 10 things that matter most

1. **The in-game hint gives away the solution.** The line under the title states the answer in 13 of the 18 non-boss levels. The text comes from `SpatialNextLessons` (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:19-21`) and `NewPlus(...)` (`COgheSpatialPlusBuilder.cs:24-31`), and is drawn by `COgheDayLabPresentation.cs:147`. Examples:
   - L12: "Khối cao vào trước, khối thấp vào sau."
   - L27: "Nhịp xa vào trước."
   - L24: "Đưa bến vào tầm đu trước."
   - L18: "Một phần giữ nút A, phần kia chốt cửa bằng B."

   The rules ask for operation hints that fade (`Docs/COGHE_LEVEL_DESIGN_RULES.md` §1.8), and the dossiers say "không gợi ý lời giải". This one string removes the "aha" from L11, L12, L13, L14, L18, L19, L21, L23, L24, L25, L27, L28 and L29. Fixing the text is the cheapest high-impact fix in the range.

2. **Each "unlock" level gives away the next level's idea.** `PLACEMENT.md` states the policy: "trước mỗi màn nặng có một màn 'mở khoá' ý khó nhất của nó với ít bước hơn". So the heavier level that follows replays an idea the player already solved:
   - L15 (E02) → L16 (content 15): go up a tube, pull A, come back, take the other route.
   - L18 (E03) → L19 (content 17) → L20 (boss): hold pad A, the partner goes through, pulls B, the holder leaves.
   - L13 (E01) → L28 (content 13). The E01 builder comment says "Level 13's idea without loading the crate; mirrored" (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:73-74`).

   This is the "levels repeat the previous one" complaint.

3. **One latch layout is cloned four times.** "Pad A holds, B latches" uses the identical `COgheLoadLatch` settings (Inputs = {pad A}, Rails = {B}, Any = true, Retain = false):
   - E03: `COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:133`
   - content 17: `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:409`
   - content 20: `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:469`
   - content 26 (L38): `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:667`

   E03 even copies content 17's spawn, camera (44,20,0), partition x = .05 and exit coordinates (compare `COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:124-126` with `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:393-396`). L30 (B1) uses the same "one half stands on pad A, the other works B" pattern. This is the "around L19–20 the layouts repeat" complaint.

4. **Neither boss is the climax of its chapter.**
   - L20 is L19 plus a crate step and a winch that needs the whole body.
   - L30 uses none of chapter 3's rope, counterweight or seesaw (L22–L26). Its split exists only to feed a "stand on pad A" AND gate.

5. **Strength matters in only two levels (L20 winch D, L30 block C1), and the game only tells you after you fail.**
   - The pull animation is identical whether the pull succeeds or not: constant stretch 1.16 (`VenomLifeAnimation.cs:285`).
   - The object does not move at all, because static friction holds it.
   - After `StallSeconds` (5–6 s) a generic line appears: "Cơ quan bị kẹt hoặc phần này chưa đủ lực" (`COgheTapRail.cs:230`).
   - `AppliedEffort` is computed (`COgheTapRail.cs:239`), but no presentation code reads it. Only `COgheCooperativeDrive` uses it, at line 78.

6. **L13's black button is the lift's "A lower call panel".** It is an unlabelled, dark-metal, collider-less puck that never animates. At level start it does the same job as the blue button on the tray. Pressing it makes COghe walk away from it, and while COghe holds the crate it is ignored completely. Details in §3.1.

7. **L18's door logic works in code but nothing shows it.**
   - B is a one-way slider that keeps the door open through an OR.
   - When B is pulled, the door is already open, so nothing on screen changes. There is no pin, pawl, sound or message.
   - The runtime already has the component the producer describes, `COgheSpringAccessDoor`. Details in §3.2.

8. **The level-number plaque hides key objects at the default camera.** It is placed on the outside of the front glass, top-left (`COgheSpecimenPlateBuilder.cs:44`).
   - It hides rope ring A and the gripping body in L22 and L24 (`18-start/action.png`, `19-start/action.png`).
   - It covers handle A or block A in L11, L12, L27 and L29, pad A in L17, crate B in L21, plank A in L26, and the C1/C2 stack in L30.

9. **Tubes respond only to taps on their mouths.** Only a tap within 1.8 × r (6.8 cm) of a mouth (`COgheTubeNetwork.cs:571-581`, `:689`), or on the first third of a branch at a junction (`:668-682`), does anything.
   - Tapping the tube body does nothing, with no marker or message (`VenomCampaign.cs:694`).
   - A closed branch is refused silently (`COgheTubeNetwork.cs:276`).
   - Tube levels in range: L14, L15, L16, L19, L20. Details in §3.3.

10. **Several levels where order could matter don't use it.** E04's dossier says "Làm theo thứ tự nào cũng được" and E07's says "đẩy B trước hay kéo A trước đều giải được". The one great order puzzle in the range, L12, is never developed in the next level. Its real successor, L27, comes 15 levels later, and its key idea is printed in the hint.

## 2. Level by level

Each level has five parts:
- **Facts:** name, content key, role, parts, mechanisms, reference route, taps.
- **Aha:** the player's moment of insight, if any.
- **New vs previous.**
- **Readability and logic problems.**
- **Proposal:** a minimum fix if the level stays, then the recommended redesign for that position (matching the curves in §4).

### L11 (content 11) · Kê một bậc

- **Facts.** Rest level after the L10 boss, 1 part. A 16 cm crate on a rail is pushed into a socket beside a slick 16 cm plinth (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:917-922`).
  - Route (`COgheSpatialNextScenario.cs:218-224`): operate A (crate to socket) → climb crate → step onto plinth → exit.
  - 4 taps, 8.5 s.
- **Aha.** Weak: "a block can be a step". Pilot L7 "Ghép một nhịp" already taught pushing a block into place to make a path. The hint "Đẩy thùng A sát bệ để làm bậc." states the full answer.
- **New vs previous.** Nothing new after a boss, which breaks the producer's rule that a boss is followed by a new-mechanism tutorial. It repeats pilot block pushing.
- **Readability.** The "11" plaque sits over the crate's front face and its blue handle (`11-start.png`).
- **Proposal.**
  - *Minimum:* set the hint to the operation only ("Chạm tay nắm để đẩy/kéo"), and move the plaque or the crate.
  - *Recommended:* make L11 the **Q tutorial** by moving content 16 here from L17 (see L17 below), so the game's signature "wow" arrives 6 levels earlier. Retire content 11 or fold it into L12; its idea duplicates pilot L7.

### L12 (content 12) · Khối lớn đi trước

- **Facts.** 1 part. The tall block A (6 cm) and the low block B (3 cm) ride perpendicular rails that cross. B starts parked in the crossing. Only 3 cm risers are climbable (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:299-309`).
  - Route (`:225-234`): pull B aside to the waiting bay → push A through to the island → push B back to A's foot → climb B → A → island → exit.
  - 7 taps, 22 s.
- **Aha.** Real: "the blocker has to step aside, then come back as the first stair". This is the producer's favourite. The hint "Khối cao vào trước, khối thấp vào sau." gives it away.
- **New vs previous.** New: ordering, plus a single block playing two roles.
- **Readability.** The "12" plaque covers block A and its handle (`12-start.png`). Otherwise clean: the outlined waiting bay is visible and the B label is clear.
- **Proposal.** Keep it.
  - Hint becomes operation only. Clear the plaque.
  - Optional: show the riser rule with a small visual (risers ≤ 3.5 cm climbable).
  - This becomes the template for the order thread: L13 adds Q to it, L19 reuses it, L27 deepens it (§6).

### L13 (E01) · Thang chở hàng

- **Facts.** "Practice (easy)", 1 part. The crate already sits on the lift tray (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:73-96`).
  - Route (`COgheSpatialPlusScenario.cs:38-54`): tap the blue A panel on the tray (board and ride up) → step back → grasp the crate → push it into the socket by the exit bench (pawl) → climb → exit.
  - 7 taps, 14 s.
- **Aha.** None. It is content 13 (L28) mirrored without the loading step (builder comment at `:73-74`).
- **New vs previous.** L12 → L13 drops order thinking and goes back to a two-step errand.
- **Readability.**
  - The **black button** (§3.1).
  - The 3 cm crate is ivory on an ivory tray and barely reads as an object (`13-E01-start.png`).
  - The hint states the solution.
- **Proposal.**
  - *Minimum:* fix the call panels (§3.1). Release a held prop automatically when a mechanism is tapped, or tell the player "Buông vật trước". Rewrite the hint.
  - *Recommended:* replace it with **"Chặn bằng lò xo" (L12 + Q)**, the logical next step from L12. Same crossing rails as L12, but B is spring-loaded back into the crossing, so it cannot be parked aside.
    - Chain:
      1. Tap Q → two halves.
      2. Half 1 pulls and holds B aside. Visible spring stretches.
      3. Half 2 pushes A through the crossing to the island.
      4. Half 1 lets go; B springs back to A's foot and becomes the low step.
      5. Merge, climb, exit.
    - Idea: "I can't hold the blocker and push at the same time, so I split." Q becomes the answer to an obstacle one body cannot solve.
    - Components: `COgheTapRail.HoldAtEnd` + `ReturnForce` (`COgheTapRail.cs:24-25`, return applied at `:185-186`), `COgheRailSlider`, `COgheQuantumSplitter`, pads not needed.
    - **Does not exist yet:** spring artwork on a rail block. `COgheSpringAccessDoor.Spring` scales a spring visual for doors only (`COgheSpringAccessDoor.cs:70`).
    - Target: 5 decisions, about 8–9 taps.

### L14 (content 14) · Luồn một vòng

- **Facts.** Tube intro, 1 part. Pulling A lifts the cap of a clear U tube over a slick partition (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:331-342`).
  - Route (`:246-251`): operate A → tap the tube mouth → flow through → exit.
  - 3 taps, 15.7 s.
- **Aha.** The body flowing through the tube is a presentation wow, not a puzzle. That is fine for a tutorial.
- **New vs previous.** A new mechanism. In the current order it is the second new system in four levels after the L10 boss (lift handling at L13 is not new).
- **Readability.**
  - The pale cyan glass U tube almost disappears against the pale floor and glass (`14-start.png`).
  - COghe spawns on top of handle A.
  - The plaque sits between the cap and the handle.
  - Tapping the tube arch does nothing (§3.3). The hint states the solution.
- **Proposal.** Keep it as the chapter's second tutorial.
  - Make the tube read clearly: stronger rim and outline, and a highlighted mouth.
  - Make the whole tube tappable (another agent's code task).
  - Move the spawn off the handle; hint "Chạm ống để chui".
  - Target: 3 decisions.

### L15 (E02) · Hai ống, một đích

- **Facts.** Practice, 1 part. Tube 1 climbs to a slick balcony where A lifts the cap of tube 2. Tube 2 crosses the partition (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:98-118`).
  - Route (`COgheSpatialPlusScenario.cs:55-64`): tube 1 up → pull A → tube 1 down → tube 2 across → walk under the exit → exit.
  - 6 taps, 30 s.
- **Aha.** Small: "read where a tube leads". Only one tube is open at the start, so there is no real choice.
- **New vs previous.** It pre-plays L16: go away to open your way, come back, take the other route.
- **Readability.**
  - The tube-2 cap is lavender. `PlusGate` makes it slick on every face (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:45-50`), and lavender is the "slick wall" colour, so the cap reads as part of the partition (`15-E02-start.png`).
  - The two faint tubes overlap on screen.
  - Tapping a tube body does nothing.
- **Proposal.** Replace it with **"Chuẩn bị trước khi đi" (tube + order)**.
  - Setup: the step block for room 2's slick 6 cm exit plinth rides a rail through a low slot under the partition. Its handle exists only in room 1.
  - Chain: push the block through the slot → pull A (cap opens) → enter the tube → climb → exit.
  - If the player goes through first, they see the slick plinth and have to come back through the two-way tube. That is a recoverable ordering lesson: prepare the far side before committing.
  - Components: `COgheTapRail`/`COgheRailSlider` (`NextCrate`), `ChapterTube` with `EntryBlocker` cap, a `ViewGate` cap that is *not* lavender.
  - Target: 4 decisions, 5–6 taps.

### L16 (content 15) · Gặp nhau ở ngã ba

- **Facts.** 1 part. A Y tube network. The left branch leads to a balcony with A, which latches open the door on the right branch (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:343-362`, `CaptureSurfaceCommandsWhileInside = true` at `:357`).
  - Route (`:252-258`): enter → at Y choose left → pull A → re-enter at the balcony mouth → at Y choose right → exit.
  - 6 taps, 33 s.
- **Aha.** "Detour to open your way." Right after L15, the player has already solved this.
- **Readability.**
  - Choosing the right branch while its door is shut is silently refused: `TryChoose` returns false (`COgheTubeNetwork.cs:276`) and the tap is swallowed.
  - Tapping the destination landing instead of the branch near Y is also swallowed. Only the HUD line "Chọn một nhánh nối" shows.
  - The plaque sits near the balcony.
- **Proposal.** Keep it. With the new L15 it is no longer a repeat.
  - Add feedback when a door is closed: a bump against the door plus "Cửa nhánh đang đóng".
  - Accept taps anywhere along a branch (tube agent).
  - Optional twist: make the balcony lever A two-position (`COgheTapRail.Stops`), where one stop opens the right branch and the other closes the left, so the order of operations matters.
  - Target: 5 decisions.

### L17 (content 16) · Một thành hai

- **Facts.** Q intro, 2 parts. Q splits 50/50, each half loads a pad, and the step bridge slides out until its pawl catches (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:363-387`).
  - Route (`:259-269`): split → left half to pad A, right half to pad B → bridge latch → merge → exit.
  - 8 taps, 10.6 s.
- **Aha.** The wow of the body splitting in two. The puzzle aha is mild: "two places at once".
- **Readability.**
  - Good: per-pad lock pins rise and the pawl drops (`latch.Pins`/`Pawl` at `:383-384`; shown by `COgheLoadLatch.Show`, `COgheLoadLatch.cs:72-86`). This is the only level in the range with a visible lock for each input.
  - Bad: the "16" plaque covers pad A (`16-start.png`).
- **Proposal.** Move this level to **L11** (post-boss tutorial).
  - Use the freed L17 slot for content 17, which is currently at L19.
  - Hint "Chạm Q để chia đôi".
  - Target: 4 decisions.

### L18 (E03) · Giữ cửa cho bạn

- **Facts.** Practice, 2 parts (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:120-142`).
  - Route (`COgheSpatialPlusScenario.cs:65-75`): split → holder on pad A (door rises) → worker walks through → worker pulls B → holder leaves → merge → exit.
  - 8 taps, 16 s.
- **Aha.** None. The hint dictates the solution, and the B action has no visible effect. Full analysis in §3.2.
- **New vs previous.** It pre-plays L19 almost exactly: same spawn, camera, partition and exit as content 17.
- **Readability.** The door is lavender, so it reads as a wall. B is a floor slider far from the door. Nothing visibly locks.
- **Proposal.** Replace it with **"Nửa thân không đủ sức" (strength)**.
  - Setup: same two rooms. Pad A drives a spring door. Lever B is the far-side catch. In room 2 a heavy block H carries a printed "100%" label and is the step to the exit plinth.
  - Chain:
    1. Split.
    2. Half 1 on A; the door rises.
    3. Half 2 goes through.
    4. (Tempting) Half 2 tries H: it **strains**, H creaks a few mm and fails.
    5. Half 2 pulls B; the catch pin snaps into the door with a click and the message "Cửa đã chốt".
    6. Half 1 leaves A and comes through.
    7. Merge, haul H as the whole body, climb, exit.
  - Idea: two halves to open the way, one whole body to do the work.
  - Components:
    - `COgheSpringAccessDoor` (exists: held pad opens it, far-side handle catches it, `CatchPin` and `Spring` visuals at `COgheSpringAccessDoor.cs:30,69-70`).
    - `NextCrate` with `CompensateLoad` and mass/resistance tuned so 50% fails. Half max force is about 0.34 N and whole is about 0.67 N (`AS_BUILT_2026_09_29.md`, force = 7 × part mass, `COgheTapRail.cs:236-237`).
    - `TapLabel("100%")` (exists, used in E09 at `COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:276-278`).
    - **Does not exist yet:** strain animation driven by effort, the visible creak or nudge, and the specific message (§3.4).
  - Target: 7 decisions, about 10 taps.

### L19 (content 17) · Bạn giữ, mình luồn

- **Facts.** 2 parts (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:388-412`).
  - Route (`:270-281`): split → holder on pad A (cap opens) → worker through the tube → worker pulls B (return door latched; the cap is OR-latched by B) → holder walks through → merge → exit.
  - 8 taps, 28 s.
- **Aha.** "Hold temporarily so the partner can make it permanent." The player already did this in L18.
- **Readability.** Better than L18, because B visibly lifts the return door through a `ViewLink`. But the cap's latch, the OR on line `:409`, is still invisible. The plaque covers Q (`17-start.png`). The hint states the solution.
- **Proposal.**
  - Move content 17 to **L17**, where it becomes the *first* hold-and-latch level. Give the cap a visible catch (pins or `COgheSpringAccessDoor`).
  - Use the freed **L19** slot (pre-boss) for **"Người giữ có việc thứ hai" (reuse the holder + order)**.
    - Room L: Q, pad A (holds the tube cap), and a dead-man handle H whose rail runs through the partition and holds room R's spring blocker aside (the L13 mechanism).
    - Room R: the L12 crossing and the exit plinth.
    - Chain: split → half 1 on A → half 2 through the tube → half 2 pulls latch L (cap visibly caught) → half 1 is free and now **holds H** (blocker aside) → half 2 pushes block T through → half 1 lets go (blocker springs back as the step) → half 1 comes through the tube → merge → climb → exit.
    - Idea: free the helper first, then give it a second job. Each step visibly causes the next.
    - Components: all exist (`COgheTissueSensor`, `COgheLoadLatch` or `COgheSpringAccessDoor`, `COgheTapRail.HoldAtEnd`, `ChapterTube`, `COgheQuantumSplitter`). Spring art does not exist yet.
    - Target: 9 decisions, 12–14 taps.

### L20 (content 20) · BOSS · Hai nửa một máy

- **Facts.** Boss, 2 parts (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:448-503`).
  - Route (`:296-315`):
    1. Split.
    2. One half on pad A (cap opens).
    3. The other goes around Q into the tube and up to the balcony.
    4. It pulls B: the return gate lifts and the cap is OR-latched.
    5. Down the ramp; merge.
    6. Walk around Q; push crate C to the bench.
    7. Climb; wind winch D with the whole body (60 s budget). The span rises.
    8. Cross; exit.
  - 16 taps, 31 s.
- **Aha.** Small: "merge before winding D". Nothing says D needs 100% (§3.4).
- **New vs previous.** Steps 1–5 are L19 again, one level later. That is the producer's L19–20 complaint. The only additions are a crate step (taught at L11) and a winch.
- **Readability.**
  - Crowded, with three clusters.
  - Winch D wears a neutral ink badge (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:126-127`), which gives no clue it is special.
  - The route needs intermediate taps to get around Q ("a straight line to the mouth hugs Q's back corner", scenario `:304-305`).
- **Proposal.** See §5.

### L21 (E04) · Cõng thùng

- **Facts.** Rest after the boss, 1 part. Cart A (3 cm) runs to a 12 cm slick shelf, and crate B (9 cm) slides across the cart (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:143-157`, `MountOnCarrier`).
  - Route (`COgheSpatialPlusScenario.cs:76-82`): pull A → push B → climb → exit.

- **Readability.** The plaque covers crate B and both handles (`21-E04-start.png`). The cart reads as a floor slab. The hint states the solution.

## 3. The producer's specific points

### 3.1 L13 "black button near COghe": what it is

**What it is.**
- `"A lower call panel"`, created in `PlusE01` (`Assets/_Game/Editor/COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:86-87`) as `MechanismVisual(c.Root, "A lower call panel", (.14, -.262, -.04), (.04, .010, .04), metal, Cylinder)`.
- It is 4 cm wide and stands about 1.6 cm proud of the lower landing, right beside the spawn at (.30, -.22, -.16).
- A twin, `"A upper call panel"`, sits on the upper landing (−.20, −.062, .05).
- Content 13 (now **L28**) has the same pair (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:323-324`). The old `13-start.png` shows that black disc beside COghe, so whichever "13" the producer played, the object is the same.

**Why it looks pressable but feels dead.**
- *Material:* `metal` is `Blade.mat`, metallic 0.86 (`VenomCampaignBuilder.cs:34`, `VenomExpansionBuilder.cs:84`). In the bright Day Lab it renders nearly black (`13-E01-start.png`, puck at about (435, 595)).
- *Art pass:* `DecorateSpatialMechanisms` recolours and badges only `lift.Panel`, the blue A on the tray (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:189`). Call panels get no colour, no A badge and no circuit trace.
- *No animation:* only `Panel` sinks when pressed (`COghePassengerLift.cs:55`). The call panel never moves.
- *No collider:* `MechanismVisual` deletes it (`VenomExpansionMechanismsBuilder.cs:52-56`). COghe can stand on it and nothing happens. It looks exactly like the round pressure pads (`ExpansionPad` "Amber cap", `VenomExpansionMechanismsBuilder.cs:141-148`), which are activated by *standing* on them.
- *New to the player:* pilot lifts (L8, L9) have no call panels. `CallPanels` appear only in E01, content 13, E11 and content 27 (grep). The player learned "blue button on the tray", then meets an unexplained black one.

**What it does** (`COghePassengerLift.TryTouch`, `COghePassengerLift.cs:31-45`). Tapping inside a 7 cm box around it:
- if the tray is moving, the tap is swallowed;
- if the tray is at another landing, it calls the empty tray to this landing;
- if the tray is already here (true at E01's start), it acts as the tray's own button: the selected part walks onto the tray and rides.

So at the start it duplicates the blue button. It is useful only for recovery after a fall ("Rơi xuống: gọi khay về bằng nút dưới", levels.json).

**Why it "cannot be pressed"** [inference, code-backed]:
1. Pressing it makes COghe walk away from it onto the tray, and the disc itself never reacts.
2. More seriously: if the player first grasps the crate, which is the obvious object (HUD "COghe đang kéo vật / Buông vật", `13-E01-action.png`), every mechanism tap is ignored. `TouchMechanism` returns false while `heldProp != null` (`VenomCampaignExpansion.cs:82`), and the tap becomes a push target for the crate (`VenomCampaign.cs:681`). Both the black and the blue button then look dead until "Buông vật" is pressed.

**Fix.**
- Option (a): remove the lower call panel in E01. The tray starts at the bottom, and recovery can use the upper panel plus the tray button.
- Option (b): redesign both call panels as clearly labelled wall buttons. Give them an A badge, a blue ring, a press animation (reuse the `Panel` depression), a printed trace to the lift and an arrow glyph (↑ / ↓). Place them away from the spawn.
- In both cases, auto-release a held prop when a mechanism is tapped, or show "Buông vật trước".
- Give the panels a real puzzle job in L28 (ferry order, §2 L28).

### 3.2 L18 (E03): the exact current logic and what is missing

**Current wiring** (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:122-142`).
- Pad A: `ExpansionPad(...,.009f)`. It is active with at least 9 g, which is 3 particles. Any part works.
- Door: `PlusGate`, slick on every face (lavender), rising 20 cm.
- B: `ViewTask`, an 8 cm floor slider in the right room, with `OneWay = true` (`:130`).
- `COgheLoadLatch "A holds, B latches the door"`: Inputs = {A}, Rails = {B}, Output = door, **Any = true (OR)**, **Retain = false (no pawl)**, Clearance = door safety (`:131-133`).
- Each step, the latch drives the door toward open whenever **pad A is loaded OR B's rail is at its end**, otherwise toward closed (`COgheLoadLatch.cs:56-69`). The clearance volume keeps it from closing on tissue.
- Because B is one-way, once at its end it ignores taps and can never return (`COgheTapRail.cs:107`). So "B at end" is a permanent second reason for the door to stay open.

**Result.** The door *is* functionally locked open from the moment B reaches its end, and the holder may then leave. But:
- When B is pulled, the door is already up because A holds it. **Nothing changes on screen.**
- No `Pins` or `Pawl` are assigned, unlike L17, so `Show` has nothing to move (`COgheLoadLatch.cs:72-86`).
- `Activity` prints nothing for Any = true (`:27-28`). There is no sound.
- B is a sideways floor slider about 20 cm from the door. Its only link is a printed coral line. The player has no evidence that the door is now locked, or that it is safe to step off A. Only the hint text says so.
- The door is lavender, the "slick wall" colour, so it does not even read as a door.

**What is missing.** A visible, audible catch *on the door* that engages at the moment B completes, plus a message to the holder ("Cửa đã chốt — có thể rời A").

**Recommended implementation (existing runtime).** Swap the OR latch for `COgheSpringAccessDoor` (`Assets/_Game/Venom/Runtime/COgheSpringAccessDoor.cs`), which already implements the producer's description. It is used by the old V2 campaign in `VenomCampaign40CooperationBuilder.cs:56-58`.
- `Input` = pad A, which drives the door up against a spring.
- `LatchHandle` = B. It is locked until the door is fully up (`:33`): you can only engage the catch on a raised door, which is logical.
- `Caught` when the door and B are both at their ends (`:30`). Then `Door.Locked`, and `CatchPin` slides 1.4 cm into the door (`:69`).
- `Spring` stretches with door travel (`:70`).
- Move B onto the partition, right beside the door on the far side, so cause and effect are adjacent.
- Give the door an ivory or A-coloured identity band, not lavender. It must stay non-climbable: keep slick faces but add a coloured frame.
- **Does not exist yet:**
  - an `Activity` string and click sound in `COgheSpringAccessDoor` (`COgheCooperativeDrive` already has "Chốt đã gài" and an `AudioSource` click, `COgheCooperativeDrive.cs:31`, `:44`);
  - a pad-side indicator that turns to "free to leave".

**Alternative.** Keep `COgheLoadLatch` but add a flag such as "catch only when input k is active" plus `Pins`/`Pawl`. That flag does not exist: with Any = true and Retain = true, A alone would latch the door.

## 5. Bosses: what each combines and its trick

### 5.1 L20 · Hai nửa một máy (rework)

- **Combines:** Q (L11), the hold → catch across rooms (L17), the strength rule (L18), order (L12–L13), tubes (L14–L16).
- **Trick:** a state flip. The player splits to open the way, must **merge** to wind a 100% winch, and must then **split again** at a second Q on the exit landing, where the exit iris needs two pads at once (an L11 callback). Planning which body state each lock needs is the climax.
- **Chain:**
  1. Q1 split.
  2. Half 1 on pad A; the tube cap rises.
  3. Half 2 goes through the tube to the balcony.

## 6. L12-style ordering opportunities

"Pull this out first, push that in later."

1. **L12** (keep). Remove the spoiler hint; it is the reference ordering puzzle.
2. **L13** (new). The same crossing with a spring blocker: the order is "hold aside → push → release", which forces a split.
3. **L15** (new). Push the step block through the partition slot *before* committing to the tube.
4. **L16** (optional). A two-stop balcony lever (`COgheTapRail.Stops`): the order of opening and closing branches matters.

11. **L29** (E07 rework). Push the top block to the far end before docking the base; an overhang punishes the wrong order.

13. **Reversal pattern, all of the above.** `COgheExitRailLock` (`COgheExitRailLock.cs`) opens an exit only while a rail is at a given stop. That allows "the block you climbed must be pulled back out, because it blocks the hatch": a use-then-remove twist with no new code.
