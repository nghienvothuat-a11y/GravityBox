## 0. Summary of findings

1. **Almost every level shows its own solution as a permanent on-screen hint.** Every non-boss level prints `Definition.Lesson` under the title (`COgheDayLabPresentation.cs:144-148`; also `VenomCampaign.cs:740`), and in most of these levels the text is the solution itself:
   - L34 "Vào Q lần nữa để chia tiếp."
   - L35 "Phần lớn đẩy, phần nhỏ giữ."
   - L39 "Bốn phần, bốn trạm."
   - L44 "Khớp tầng dưới trước, rồi tới tầng trên."
   - L45 "Ráp đủ khung rồi mới nâng."

   The strings are in `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:19-21` and `COgheSpatialPlusBuilder.cs:32-43`. As long as these hints stay, no level can make the player feel clever. This is the cheapest fix on the list.
2. **Five templates make up most of 31–50:**
   - **"Hold → latch → friend follows"** appears in 9 of the 20 levels: L33, L37, L38, L39, L40 (twice), L43, L46, L49, L50.
   - **"Merge, then whole-body winch"** closes L40 and L45. It already closed L20 and, as a whole-body haul, L30.
   - **"Far piece first"** appears in L40 and L45, after L27.
   - **"Q split, then N pads together slide a step out"** is L34 and L42, after L17.
   - **Several levels are near-clones:**
     - L32 = L16 (the reference scripts are line-for-line identical).
     - L47 = L31 (same layout, different output).
     - L48 = L41.
     - L37 ≈ L39 ≈ L46 (L37 and L39 share the same lift, platform and latch coordinates).
     - L44 ≈ L49 ≈ L50 (one shared tower function).
3. **Bosses copy the level before them.** L50 (B2) is L49 (E18) plus extra parts: both call `PlusTower`, with the same Q position, spawn and carriage A/B coordinates (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:506-591`). L40 (30) strings together earlier ideas without a single trick of its own.
4. **The gear tutorial is in the wrong chapter.** L31 introduces gears, but no other level in chapter 4 (L32–L40) uses them. Gears come back at L41, ten levels later. At L31 the gears do nothing a plain pad could not do.
5. **Mass is a key puzzle input in only 3–4 of the 20 levels, and the game shows it badly:**
   - It matters in L33 (heavy pad), L35 (heavy block), and the whole-body winches of L40 and L45.
   - There is no strain animation. A part that is too weak just stalls, and after `StallSeconds` a text appears: "Cơ quan bị kẹt hoặc phần này chưa đủ lực" (`COgheTapRail.cs:229-230`). `AppliedEffort` is computed but no Spatial presentation reads it.
   - Gear motor pads use threshold 0.009 kg (≈3 particles), so a 25% sliver drives a machine exactly as the whole body does (`COgheGearTrain.cs:28-36,103`). This contradicts the "small parts are weak" rule taught in L35 and L45.
6. **The locks that hold doors open are hidden or tiny:**
   - L40's bolts sit *inside* the hollow centre deck (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:860-861`).
   - L45's frame-lock bolt is inside the landing (`:828`).
   - L33's door pin is a 2 cm cube about 46 cm away from the door it pins (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:274,279`).
   - L37/L39's lift-enable pin is a 1.2 cm cube.

   Only L34 shows its lock state properly (pins plus pawl beside the step).
7. **Tubes only accept taps on an open mouth.** From outside, only an open Entry node within 1.8×r (≈6.8 cm) of the tap ray is picked (`COgheTubeNetwork.cs:571-581,684-690`). A tap on the tube body falls through to whatever surface is behind it. A branch can be chosen only after the body has stopped in the junction, and only by tapping the first third of that branch (`:266-271,668-682`). Six of the 20 levels use tubes: L32, L36, L38, L40, L42, L43.
8. **High tap counts here mostly mean tedium.** L42 (23 taps) and L46 (27) each contain about one real decision. The reference scripts need many re-taps because a tap whose ray passes over another part selects that part instead (AS_BUILT "Chạm có tia đi qua một phần khác sẽ chọn phần đó"; retry loops at `COgheSpatialNextScenario.cs:123-133`). That is a crowding and input problem, not difficulty.
9. **Several things look pressable but are not, or hide things that are:**
   - The Q output trays are printed squares labelled "50%" (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:169-170`). They look like pads, and in L33 they sit beside a real pad that is also labelled "50%".
   - Junction "valve selector" cubes look like controls but are not.
   - Pulley drums look interactive.
   - The level number plates cover tap targets: the rope ring in L36, winch B in L45, the gear column in L44, L49 and L50.
   - The useful labels ("tầng 1/2/3", "máy 1/2", the ghost slot for G) exist only in the illustrations. `PlusLabel`/`PlusGhost` are design notes, not scene objects (`COgheSpatialPlusBuilder.cs:49-53`).
10. **Proposed direction:**
    - Chapter 4 (31–40) becomes **"Weight & order"**. L31 teaches strength with a new strain/weight-plate presentation. The chapter then builds L12-style ordering under weight constraints and ends in a boss whose trick is an *exact-weight* balance: heavier is no longer better.
    - Chapter 5 (41–50) stays **"Gears"** but turns them into reasoning puzzles: crossing gear carriages (L12 in gears), direction reversal with an idler gear, motors whose force depends on the weight of the part driving them, and reusing a gear once its output has latched. It ends in a gearbox boss.
    - About half of the proposals need only builder changes. The rest need a short list of new runtime or presentation features (section 6).

### 1.3 Mass and strength: where it matters and how the player is told

- Pads activate at `COgheTissueSensor.Threshold` (`COgheTissueSensor.cs:11-14`). The cap sinks 6 mm and a lamp turns green; the display is on/off only, with no gauge.

### 1.5 Tubes: current tap behaviour and confusion

The runtime rules, in `COgheTubeNetwork.cs`:
- **Outside a tube:** `TryTouch` (`:252-265`) calls `PickVisibleEntry` (`:571-581`), which considers only *open Entry terminals* within `Radius*1.8` of the tap ray (`RayScore` `:684-690`). A tap on the tube body is ignored by the tube and lands on the surface behind it.
- **Inside a tube:** taps are swallowed when `CaptureSurfaceCommandsWhileInside` is set (all levels here).
- **At a junction:** the group must stop and wait ("Chọn một nhánh nối"). `PickAdjacent` (`:668-682`) only samples the first third of each branch, so a destination cannot be pre-selected. Every junction costs a stop and a tap.

## 2. Level by level

For each level: what it is now, its "aha", what it repeats, its problems, and a redesign proposal. "Exists" means an existing runtime component or builder helper; **NEW** means it does not exist yet.

### L31 (E08) · Bánh răng đầu tiên ("First gear")

**Now.**
- Role: rest after the boss, plus gear intro. 1 part. Mechanism: `COgheGearTrain` with pad A and a rising deck (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:249-265`).
- Chain (`COgheSpatialPlusScenario.cs:127-134`): stand on A → deck rises and latches → climb stairs → cross the deck → exit.
- 4 taps, 0 decisions.

**Aha.** None. The lesson text tells you to stand on A.

**New vs previous.** Visually new (gears). Functionally it is a pad raising a step, which players have done since L17/L18. L47 reuses the same layout. Gears then disappear for nine levels.

**Problems.**
- The gears are a cosmetic middleman. The motor pad threshold is 0.009, so the claim that the gears "do the heavy work for me" is not something the player can observe.
- The deck sits in a pit between the ledges and its raise is hard to read from the start view (31-E08-start.png).

**Redesign → "Một nửa không nổi" (A half can't budge it): chapter 4 tutorial for weight and strength. Gears move to L41.**
1. Tap Q → 50% + 50% (HUD chips).
2. Each half steps onto pads A1/A2. A shared latch lifts gate G in front of crate H; its pawl visibly snaps, so both halves may leave.
3. A half taps H, which carries a ●●●● weight plate. The body braces and stretches, H shudders, the plate shows ●●○○ in red, and the task ends.
4. Tap between the halves → they merge.
5. The whole body slides H into its socket, where H becomes a step.
6. Climb → exit.

- Exists: `COgheQuantumSplitter`; `ExpansionPad`/`COgheTissueSensor`; `COgheLoadLatch` with `Pins`/`Pawl` (pattern `Next24`, `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:601-607`); `NextCrate` with heavy tuning like `Next25`'s B (`:619-620`: mass, resistance, `CompensateLoad`, `StallSeconds`).
- **NEW:** a strain presentation that reads `COgheTapRail.AppliedEffort`, `Rail.Effort` and the stall; and a weight-plate graphic.
- Teaches: whole-body strength, and the visible pawl means you can let go.
- Target: 5 decisions, about 8 taps.

### L32 (23) · Đổi tuyến trên vách ("Switch the route on the wall")

**Now.**
- Role: tube plus high mechanism. 1 part. Mechanisms: `COgheTubeNetwork` with valve `AccessGate`, and handle A (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:556-576`).
- Chain (`COgheSpatialNextScenario.cs:336-342`): enter → at J choose the ledge branch → pull A (opens the J→high-tray branch) → re-enter from the ledge → at J choose up → exit.
- 6 taps, 1 decision.

**Aha.** The dossier's aha ("the two tubes only cross, they don't join") is undercut: the lesson text gives the order, and the solution is identical to L16.

**New vs previous.** Nothing new. It is a copy of L16 with a crossing drawn in (see 1.1).

**Problems.**
- Low-contrast tubes and mouths.
- The junction selector cube looks like the control, while the real control is A on the far ledge, linked only by a faint trace (23-start.png).
- It has no connection to chapter 4 ("Nhiều phần cùng làm").

**Redesign → "Nặng đi trước" (Heavy goes first): the L12 crossing with weight. Drop the tube content, which duplicates L16.**

Layout: the crossing rails of `Next12` (`:301-309`). Tall block T is heavy (●●●●) and is parked **in the crossing**. Light block L sits in its bay; its rail is bolted by pad P, with an `InterlockPin` drawn on L's rail. Final stair: floor → L (3 cm) → T (6 cm) → island.
1. Whole body pushes T out of the crossing to its side bay. This only works while whole: the heavy job comes first.
2. Q → 50/50.
3. Half 1 holds P → L's pin lifts.
4. Half 2 pulls L through the crossing to its far socket (catch visible).
5. Merge (L stays docked).
6. Whole body pushes T back to its crossing seat.
7. Climb L → T → island → exit.

If the player splits first, L hits T and the halves strain on T. That is a recoverable lesson: merge, move T, split again.
- Exists: `NextCrate` ×2; heavy tuning as in L31; `RequiredRail` bolt (`Next25` `:621-625`); `COgheTapRail.InterlockPin`.
- NEW: shared with L31 (strain, plate).
- Teaches: planning *when* to split.
- Target: 6–7 decisions, about 10 taps.
- If a tube level is wanted here instead, see L36.

### L33 (E09) · Đủ nặng mới mở ("Heavy enough to open")

**Now.**
- Role: practice (easy–medium). Up to 3 parts. Mechanisms: Q, threshold pad A (0.030), light pads B1/B2, door, door pin (`COgheSpatialPlusLevels.cs:269-291`).
- Chain (`COgheSpatialPlusScenario.cs:135-146`): Q → 50% onto A (door rises) → Q the other half → 25% onto B1 and B2 (pin catches) → merge → exit.
- 9 taps, 1 decision.

**Aha.** None. The floor labels "50%" and "25%" map one-to-one onto the parts, and the lesson text repeats it.

**New vs previous.** The weight threshold is new. The layout is the L18 (E03) hold-door level, and the dossier says so ("Như màn 18 mới").

**Problems.**
- The pin that keeps the door open is a 2 cm cube about 46 cm away from the door (`:274` door z −0.20, `:279` pin z 0.26).
- The Q trays are also labelled "50%" (33-E09-action.png shows three "50%" squares).
- There is no load gauge and no "too light" response.

**Redesign → "Hai phần tư thành một nửa" (Two quarters make a half).**
1. Q → 50/50, then Q one half → 50/25/25.
2. The 50% stands on door pad H (●● gauge fills) → door D rises, held only while H is loaded.
3. Both quarters go in. Two light pads ● ● (AND) raise an inner hatch that latches, with a visible pawl.
4. Behind the hatch is pad K (●●), which pins door D. One quarter on K shows a half-filled gauge and nothing happens. **Aha:** walk the second quarter onto K; they merge, the gauge fills, and a pin slides into D's frame.
5. The 50% leaves H; D stays pinned. The 50% walks in, everyone merges, exit.

- Exists: Q; thresholds as now; `COgheLoadLatch` AND + Retain for the hatch; `COgheLoadLatch` Any + `Clearance` for D (as now, but with the pin drawn on D).
- **NEW:** a gauge presentation for `COgheTissueSensor` (`Load` is already public, `COgheTissueSensor.cs:13`).
- Teaches: split for an AND, merge for weight.
- Target: 7 decisions, about 11 taps.

### L34 (24) · Hai rồi bốn ("Two, then four")

**Now.**
- Role: introduces repeated splitting. 4 parts. Mechanisms: Q, 4 pads (0.009), drawer step with pins and pawl (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:596-610`).
- Chain (`COgheSpatialNextScenario.cs:343-358`): Q → Q → Q → four quarters on A1/A2/B1/B2 → step catches → merge → climb → exit.
- 15 taps, 1 decision.

**Aha.** None. The lesson text says "go into Q again".

**New vs previous.** It is L17 with four pads instead of two; L42 repeats it again.

**Problems.**
- Mass is irrelevant (all pads 0.009) even though the HUD shows 25% chips.
- About 10 of the 15 taps are walking chores.
- This is the one level with a well-shown lock (pins plus pawl; 24-action.png). Keep that part.

**Redesign → "Bập bênh nâng bạn" (The seesaw lifts your friend): weight as a counterweight.**
1. Q → 50/50 → Q → 50/25/25.
2. Quarter q1 walks onto the low end of seesaw S (at floor level).

## 4. Bosses

### L40 · BOSS · "Cân ba phần tư" (The three-quarter balance)

**Combines:** L31 (strength), L32 (heavy first), L35 (75/25), L34 (weight shown by motion), visible locks. **Trick:** for the whole chapter heavier was better. This balance accepts only exactly ●●●. With 100% the beam tips the wrong way, and with 50% it rises.

1. In the right bay, a heavy block T (●●●●) is parked in the crossing at the foot of the balance stair. The whole body must push it to its seat before splitting (the heavy job first).
2. Q → 50/50 → Q → 50/25/25 → walk one 25% into the 50% → 75% + 25%.
3. The 25% stands on pad P in the left bay, separated by a slick wall so it cannot merge back. The balance's pivot-lock pin visibly lifts; the beam is locked until then, so it cannot be tipped by accident.
4. The 75% walks onto the balance pan → the beam settles level against a fixed ●●● counterweight → the pawl catches and the beam becomes the bridge.
5. The 25% leaves P (the beam stays caught) and crosses; everyone merges on the far side → exit.

The likely first attempt (100% on the pan) visibly fails and sends the player back to Q, which stays reachable.

- Components: Q; pads; `COgheLoadLatch` with `Pins`/`Pawl`; heavy `NextCrate`.
- **NEW:** a two-pan balance with a level catch. Prototype it from `COgheSeesawBridge`'s level pawl or `PlusSeesaw`'s hinge physics; tissue weight tipping a free plank is already proven in L26/E06.
- Target: 10–11 decisions, about 15 taps (vs 29 now).
- It is not a copy of L39: different room, different trick.

### L50 · BOSS · "Hộp số" (The gearbox)

**Combines:** L42/L47 (carriage ordering), L44 (idler reversal), L45 and L49 (weight-scaled motors), L43 (selector), L48 (layers). **Trick:** the idler you inserted to open the stairs must later come out, and the heaviest part must drive the final stage.

1. Q → 50/50 → Q → 50/25/25.
2. q1 on motor pad P. With selector S at "stairs" and no idler, the floor train opens the stair gate (light load); its pawl snaps.
3. The 50% and q2 climb. q2 pushes idler I into the mid-layer train → the direction arrows flip, needed for the exit shutter's rack.
4. q2 moves S to "shutter" → the motor now drives the shutter, but q1 alone strains (the shutter is heavy: gauge ●○○○ of ●●●○).
5. **Aha:** the 50% goes back down and merges with q1 → 75% on P → the shutter rises and latches.
6. Second ordering beat: the deck bridge to the exit shares the mid-layer train and needs the *original* direction, so q2 must pull I back out (pull out after push in) and the 75% runs P once more → the bridge rises.
7. Everyone climbs → merge → exit.

- Exists: `COgheViewTransmission` (selector), `PlusGearCarriage`, `ShaftLinks`, Q, pads.
- **NEW:** signed rack (L44); load-scaled motor (L45); strain on gears.
- Target: 11–12 decisions, about 18 taps.
- New layout. Do not reuse `PlusTower`.

## 5. Ordering-puzzle opportunities (developing L12)

What makes L12 work: the blocks are visually simple, they physically block each other, every move can be undone, and the right order has to be reasoned out (`Next12`, `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:301-309`).

Primitives that already support this:
- `NextCrate` rails that collide.
- `LatchAtStart`/`LatchAtEnd`, with a reverse pull releasing a catch (`COgheRailSlider.cs:47-49`).
- `COgheTapRail.Stops` for multi-position handles.
- `RequiredRail`/`RequiredPosition` cams (`COgheTapRail.cs:30-34,61-63`).
- Gear carriages, which are rails as well.

Where to use them:
1. **L32 "Nặng đi trước":** L12 plus weight; the order of splitting is part of the order of moves.
2. **L39 (content 29):** far piece first, kept but made readable; it is now the only far-first level in the chapter.
3. **L42 "Hai xe chéo nhau":** L12 with gear carriages.
4. **L44 idler:** the order of inserting the idler decides which output moves.
5. **L47 "Mượn bánh":** take a gear out only after its output has latched.
6. **L50 boss:** insert, then remove the same gear.

Further ideas, not yet placed:
- **"Three blocks, two lanes":** a 3-piece version of L27 where one piece must be parked *temporarily in the final seat of another* and pulled out again. Builder only.
- **"Coupled rails":** one handle moves block X forward *and* block Y back through a `ViewLink` (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:664` pattern), so moves have side effects the player must plan around. Builder only.
- **"Gauge order":** pads whose required weight forces a split/merge sequence (L33 pattern) behind one-way flaps, so the order of merging becomes the puzzle.
