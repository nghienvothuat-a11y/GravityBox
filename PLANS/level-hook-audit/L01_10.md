## 0. Summary

1. **Few real decisions.** Counting the mechanism operations a player must choose, today's levels go 0, 0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 3. In levels 4–8, five levels in a row, the whole solution is one tap on the only handle or button in the box. The code supports the producer's point that these are not brain-teasers.
2. **The repetition is literal in code.**
   - L4 and L5 run the same code path (`if(n==4||n==5)`, builder 87-94) and have the same solution: `Operate("A")`, then the exit (scenario 21).
   - L6, L9 and L10 all call `SpatialPulley` with the same coordinates (builder 96, 126-136). The only change is that L10's receiving plinth is deeper (129).
   - L10 is the L9 layout plus the L8 lift (`SpatialLift`, builder 98).
3. **Order comes from reachability, not reasoning.** Any tap is auto-routed across the whole navigation graph: `VenomCampaignMotion.Move` calls `FindPath` (`VenomCampaignMotion.cs:197-217`), and handle taps path-find too (`COgheTapRail.cs:133-135`). The intermediate "Go" taps in the scenario make the author replay robust; they are not player decisions. *Inference:* once a route exists, a player can tap the far target directly. For example, L6 likely takes 2 taps, not 5.
4. **The on-screen lesson gives the answer away.** The lines in `SpatialLessons` (builder 17) appear under the title (`COgheDayLabPresentation.cs:144-147`). Examples: L7 "Chạm tay B để đưa nhịp cầu vào chỗ trống"; L9 "Nâng nhịp A, rồi tìm tay B".
5. **The boss under-delivers.** L10 opens with a "WARNING · VERY HARD LEVEL" tour (`Runtime/Boss/COgheBossIntro.cs:9-13`). It then takes 6 taps with 3 forced operations, the same tap count as L9 (PLACEMENT.md).
6. **Proposal.** Add one new idea and one more step per level, and make every unlock visible: guard → stop pin → lift round trip → bridge with two jobs. The boss then has 5–6 operations. Its trick is to use learned devices backwards: the lift goes *down*, and the camera orbit from L3 reveals a hidden jam pin. Curve in §4; boss in §3.

## 1. Cross-cutting issues (several levels)

**C1. Lavender (slippery) is never taught, and it looks inconsistent.**
- L1–L5 contain no slippery surface: the shell only gets the slippery flag for `n>=6` (builder 82, 124).
- The first lavender faces are L6's receiving-plinth sides (builder 129), shown without introduction.
- In L6 and L8–L10 the slippery glass walls are panes larger than 0.4 m. Those get the almost clear "Expansion satin shell" (`COgheExpansionArtBuilder.cs:30, 50-51`), so they look like L5's climbable glass.
- L7's 0.34 m panes get the strong "Ice satin coating" instead, so the whole box turns lavender and contrast drops (`07-start.png`).

**C2. State and links are hard to read.**
- A door is an ivory plate with a 10 mm colour band and a badge (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:117-118`).
- A link is a 3 mm printed trace (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:238`).
- `COgheViewMechanism` has a door lamp (`Lamp/Waiting/Ready`, `COgheViewMechanism.cs:12-13, 30`). It is wired only in the older View art (`COgheViewArtBuilder.cs:163`), never in Spatial.
- "Locked" or "blocked" feedback is text in the status line only (`COgheTapRail.cs:64-65, 128, 135`). `COgheControlFeedback` offers only `ShowCommand` (line 70); there is no visual "denied" state.
- In L4, L5, L9 and L10 the exit ring sits behind its door at the start, so the goal is not visible (start screenshots).

**C3. The level plaque hides a handle.** The plaque sits at a fixed spot on the front glass, top-left (`COgheSpecimenPlateBuilder.cs:44`). In the low L4 box it covers handle A from the default camera (`04-start.png`, both sets). It is decoration only (no collider, line 54), so taps still pass through, but the player cannot see the handle.

**C4. A/B labels carry no consistent meaning.**
- L7's only handle is labelled B (builder 97).
- L8's lift is badged A, but no door A exists (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:188-192`).
- L10 has three coral "B" objects with three different roles: a handle that powers the lift, the lift's button, and the exit door.
- There is a convention to reuse: handles not labelled A or B are drawn neutral, pearl with ink lettering (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:126`). Proposed rule:
  - **neutral C/D = unlockers**;
  - **blue A / coral B = main circuits**;
  - a door wears the colour of whatever opens it.

**C5. Jitter (producer named L4; the code side belongs to another agent).** All of this is inference, not measured per event:
- The door chases the handle through a P-controller (`COgheViewMechanism.cs:21-24`): gain 5, force clamped to ±0.45 N on a 22 g shutter.
- Every rail switches between static and kinetic friction at 2 mm/s (`COgheRailSlider.cs:57`). That is a stick-slip candidate when a handle is pulled slowly.
- The handle has two coupled controllers: the body's walk intent (`COgheTapRail.cs:92-104`) and the hand force, which applies an equal reaction to every particle (lines 231-245).
- Frame spikes on the Mac occur mainly on the pulley levels: L6, L9 and L10 have p99 of 24–33 ms (`Docs/Verification/COgheSpatialCircuits/README.md`).
- Navigation-graph rebuilds when a mechanism changes state are candidates (`COgheTapRail.cs:225`, `COgheViewMechanism.cs:32`, `COghePassengerLift.cs:82`).

### L1 · Chạm để đi (intro: tap to move)
1. **Mechanisms:** none.
   - Spawn at (-.26,-.25,-.19); exit ring on the back wall at (.23,-.225,.30) (builder 80).

2. **Aha:** none, which is right for a first tutorial.

4. **Readability:** good; the ring is visible (`01-start.png`). The ring line is 3.2 mm wide (builder 106). *Inference:* it may be thin on a phone.
5. **Redesign:** keep. **Target:** 0 operations, 1 tap.

### L2 · Leo từng bậc (intro: climbing)
1. **Mechanisms:** two fixed ivory blocks (builder 85); exit raised to y -.07 (81).
   - **Solution:** go to block 1, go to block 2, tap the exit. **3 taps** (scenario 19).
2. **Aha:** none. *Inference:* tapping the exit directly would auto-route over both blocks, so "step by step" is optional.
3. **New:** climbing. **Repeated:** nothing.
4. **Readability:** good. This is the natural place to teach lavender before L6 depends on it (C1).
5. **Redesign:** "Go around the lavender."
   - **Chain:**
     1. The face of the tall block that the player sees first is lavender (set `Slippery` on that ViewBlock face, as builder 129 does).
     2. The player walks around to an ivory face.
     3. Climb it, then exit.
   - **Components:** `VenomSurfacePatch.Slippery` (exists).
   - **Teaches:** the material language: lavender cannot be climbed.
   - **Target:** 0 operations, 1 route decision, about 2 taps.

### L3 · Nhìn quanh vách (intro: camera orbit)
1. **Mechanisms:** an L-shaped ivory wall hides the exit (builder 86); camera set to (38,0,0).

4. **Readability:** the ring sliver gives away where the exit is.
5. **Redesign:** keep it as the camera tutorial, but hide the ring completely so orbiting is actually needed. The boss proposal (§3) pays this skill off.

### L4 · Kéo là mở (intro: handle → door)
1. **Mechanisms:**

   - `COgheRailSlider` "A blue shutter" over the exit, travel .14 (93 → ViewGate).
   - `COgheViewMechanism` link with FinalGate on (ViewLink, `COgheViewCampaignBuilder.cs:196-201`).
   - **Solution:** Operate A, then the exit. **2 taps.**
2. **Aha:** none. There is one handle, and the lesson says "Tay xanh A nối với cửa xanh A."
3. **New:** handles and linked doors. **Repeated:** nothing.
4. **Readability:**
   - The handle is partly behind the "04" plaque (C3).
   - The door reads as an ivory panel, and the ring is hidden behind it (`04-start` vs `04-action`).
   - The door has no state lamp (C2).
   - The producer's stutter report: see C5.
5. **Redesign:** keep **1 operation**; an intro should be a single clean idea (design rules §1.8).
   - Move the handle or the plaque so the handle is clear.
   - Let the ring's rim show around the shutter so the goal is visible.
   - Wire the existing `Lamp`, or light the trace in step with `Input.Fraction` (presentation only).

### L5 · Tay kéo trên vách (today: L4 again, on the wall)
1. **Mechanisms:** same code path as L4.
   - `COgheTapRail` "A" on the back glass ("Outer pane 2") at (-.23,-.04,.268), stand offset (0,-.068,0) (builder 89-92).
   - Blue shutter on the raised exit, y .05 (81).
   - **Solution:** Operate A, then the exit. **2 taps.** Identical to L4 (scenario 21; PLACEMENT.md).
2. **Aha:** none. The climb is auto-routed when the handle is tapped (`COgheTapRail.cs:133-135`), so the player never decides to climb.
3. **New:** a wall-mounted handle, which is only a visual difference. **Repeated:** L4, exactly. The producer is right.
4. **Readability:** the handle is read through two panes of glass; the body hangs under it (`05-action.png`).
5. **Redesign (producer's idea):** "Open the guard first."
   - **Chain:**
     1. A neutral floor lever C next to spawn has a trace running up the back glass to a clear guard over wall handle A. Pulling C slides the guard up.
     2. Tap A. COghe climbs and pulls it; exit shutter A opens.
     3. Exit.
     Before step 1, tapping A must visibly refuse: a lock icon or shake, plus "Chốt đang khóa".
   - **Components (all exist):**
     - C = ViewTask.
     - Guard = ViewGate (`COgheRailSlider`) with clear glass material ("Clear optical glass" or "Divider glass", `COgheExpansionArtBuilder.cs:26, 31`), driven by ViewLink with FinalGate off.
     - A = ViewTask with `RequiredRail = guard`, `RequiredEnd = true`, plus `InterlockPin` (`COgheTapRail.cs:30-36, 61-63, 190`).
     - Exit = ViewGate + ViewLink with FinalGate on.
     - Precedent for "cover before handle": `COgheSequentialWinch.Access` (`COgheSequentialWinch.cs:5-8`, used at `VenomCampaign30Builder.cs:438`).
   - **Gaps:**
     - No dedicated "cover" component exists; none is needed.
     - No visual denied feedback (C2).
     - The guard's collider must not swallow the tap meant for A: `TryTouch` checks against the nearest solid (`COgheTapRail.cs:114`), and an opaque cover intercepts the command (`VenomCampaign.cs:694`).
   - **Teaches:** locks and guards; the order is readable because C's trace ends at the guard.
   - **Target:** 2 operations, 3–4 taps.

### L6 · Một sợi dây (intro: pulley bridge)
1. **Mechanisms (SpatialPulley, builder 126-136):**
   - Ivory departure plinth.
   - Receiving plinth with slippery sides (129).
   - Floor winch "A": `COgheTapRail` with `CompensateLoad` and `StallSeconds = 6` (130).
   - "Pulley lifting deck" rail, travel .16, latches at the end (131).
   - `COghePulleyDrive` cable over two wheels (132-135).
   - Exit at y -.035. The walls are slippery.
   - **Solution:** Operate A, climb, step onto the bridge, cross, exit. **5 taps.** *Inference:* 2 taps would do.
2. **Aha:** none. Pull the only handle; the route is automatic.
3. **New:** pulley and cable, a raised bridge, and the first lavender surfaces (untaught, C1). **Repeated:** L4's decision.
4. **Readability:**
   - The cable is 2.2 mm (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:176`) but does show cause and effect.
   - The crank is right beside spawn.
   - The slippery walls look climbable (C1).
5. **Redesign (producer's idea):** "Climb, unlock, crank."
   - **Chain:**
     1. Climb the departure plinth and pull neutral wall handle C on the back glass above it. Its trace runs down to a clear box around floor crank A, and the lid lifts.
     2. Crank A. The cable winds, and the bridge rises and latches.
     3. Cross, then exit.
     This is L5's grammar reversed (high first, then low).
   - **Components (all exist):** ViewTask on the glass (as in L5); ViewGate lid + ViewLink; SpatialPulley with `crank.RequiredRail = lid`.
   - **Art note:** the pulley art is hard-coded as A and blue (`COgheSpatialArtBuilder.cs:166-184`). That is fine if the crank stays A.
   - **Teaches:** an effect at a distance through a cable, and backtracking.
   - **Target:** 2 operations, 4–5 taps.

## 3. Boss L10 proposal: "Cỗ máy thân quen"

Use a **new layout**, not `SpatialPulley`'s coordinates: start floor | a deep central pit holding the pulley deck | receiving side, with the exit high on the wall behind shutter A. A lift (coral B) joins the start floor to the pit floor and **starts at the top**, its deck flush with the floor.

**Chain** (each step visibly causes the next):
1. Climb the ivory service wall and pull neutral handle C. Its trace leads to a clear guard over winch A, which lifts. *(L5/L6)*
2. Tap winch A. It refuses ("Chốt đang khóa"). A red pin tip is visible under the bridge deck; orbit to see the pin locking the deck, and its trace leading down into the pit to lever D. *(L3 camera, L7 pin)*
3. Press lift button B. It sinks and the lift goes **down** into the pit, the opposite of every earlier ride. *(L8 round trip, reversed)*
4. Pull D. The pin withdraws. *(L7)*
5. Press B again; it popped up on arrival. Ride up.
6. Crank A. The deck rises and latches, and its rail also drives exit shutter A open: the bridge is the key. *(L6)*
7. Cross, then exit.

**Trick:**
- The cause of the jam is visible only from another camera angle.
- The lift, which has only ever gone up, is the way down.

Both are "known devices used in the opposite way", and both are readable: a trace connects every lock to its lever.

**Ideas combined:** guard (L5/L6), pulley (L6), stop pin and pit (L7), lift round trip with a stateful button (L8), and camera orbit (L3).
**Size:** 5 operations + 1 discovery tap, about 8–10 taps. That sits between today's 6 and the chapter 2 boss at 16 (PLACEMENT.md).

**Components:**
- C and D = ViewTask.
- Guard and pin = ViewGate + ViewLink.
- `winch.RequiredRail = pin` (interlock, `COgheTapRail.cs:30`).
- A lift that starts at the top: `ExpansionRail(..., initial = travel)`; Board sends it down when it is at its end (`COghePassengerLift.cs:72`).
- A ViewLink from the deck rail to the exit shutter (`COgheViewMechanism` takes any `COgheRailSlider` as input).

**Gaps and risks:**
- The button presentation, as in L8.
- A seam-free lift deck at floor level (builder risk).
- If you want a real physical **jam** (the deck lifts 1 cm, then stalls), no generic "rail locks rail" component exists. Today `COgheRailSlider.Locked` is set only by specific mechanisms such as `COgheSpringAccessDoor`, `COgheSequentialWinch` and `COgheTwoStageWinch`. That would need a tiny new component, or a collider jam that must be tuned. The jam also risks reading as the stutter bug the producer already reports. **Recommendation:** use the interlock, and show the refusal immediately.

**Variant B (producer's crate idea):** replace steps 3–5. A heavy crate E on the deck covers pin lever D: slide E off, pull D, crank A.
- **Exists:** a crate on a rail, `NextCrate` (`COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:231-243`). Mechanically it is L7's slider in a new skin. A real weight stall is also possible, because the pulley's tension is capped at 1.2 N (`COghePulleyDrive.cs:13`).
- **Caveats:**
  - It drops the lift callback.
  - Position 11 ("Kê một bậc", `Next11`, `COgheSpatialNextBuilder.cs:917-921`) is the crate tutorial that follows the boss. Keep the boss's crate as a *load to remove*, never as a step, so L11 keeps its new idea.
